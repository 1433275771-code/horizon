## [Genome Language Models Generate Viable Novel Bacteriophages](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vjj4pr/r_generative_design_of_novel_bacteriophages_with/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers used the genome language models Evo 1 and Evo 2 to design whole bacteriophage genomes based on the lytic phage ΦX174, and experimentally confirmed 16 viable novel phages. This is the first demonstration of generative language models producing functional sequences at whole-genome scale. The work shows that generalist genome language models can go beyond prediction to generative design of complete, functional genomes, opening new avenues for synthetic biology and phage therapy. It also demonstrates that AI-designed organisms can exhibit substantial evolutionary novelty, suggesting a paradigm shift in how biological systems can be engineered. The models generated whole-genome sequences with realistic genetic architectures and desirable host tropism, using ΦX174—a small single-stranded DNA virus that infects E. coli—as the design template. The resulting 16 viable phages showed substantial evolutionary novelty, underscoring the generative capability of genome foundation models like Evo 2 \(trained on 9 trillion base pairs\).

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/moschles · Aug 9, 07:11

**Background**: Genome language models \(gLMs\) are large language models trained on DNA and RNA sequences, conceptualizing the genome as a biological text whose grammar encodes regulatory interactions. Evo 2 is a frontier genomic foundation model that predicts functional properties and generates sequences at single-nucleotide resolution with up to 1 million base pair context. The bacteriophage ΦX174 is a historically important, very small virus that infects E. coli, making it an ideal template for testing whole-genome design.

This milestone significantly expands China&\#x27;s AI compute capacity and raises the global scale benchmark for AI data centers, backed by a planned capacity of 2GW and a token-output-optimized design. It is the first flagship project under Envision&\#x27;s &\#x27;Gobi Mission&\#x27; plan, which aims to provide replicable solutions for domestic computing clusters.

Robots would extract minerals from lunar soil to produce AI computing satellites, which would then be launched into orbit using electromagnetic mass drivers. ## [MiniMax H3 Team AMA: Open-Sourcing 2K DiT Model and Sparse Attention](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/s/fjM3d7AEV8) ⭐️ 8.0/10

During a Reddit AMA on r/StableDiffusion, the MiniMax H3 team announced plans to open-source a 2K regeneration model \(a dedicated latent-space DiT, not a standard upscaler\) and a sparse attention reference implementation. They are also considering 4/8-step low-step versions and a standalone image generation model derived from the H3 family. This signals a major open-source push from a leading video generation team, potentially making high-resolution video generation and efficient attention techniques accessible to the broader community. The sparse attention implementation could reduce compute costs and improve quality without perceptible loss, benefiting both researchers and developers. The team did not provide a concrete release date for the H3-Regenerate-2K model. They acknowledged community feedback about quality degradation in Ref2VA and texture detail blurriness, and said improvements are already underway.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 9, 08:28

**Background**: DiT \(Diffusion Transformer\) is a diffusion model architecture that replaces the commonly used U-Net backbone with a Vision Transformer operating on latent patches, offering better scalability. Sparse attention is an optimization that computes attention over a meaningful subset of tokens rather than all token pairs, reducing the O\(N²\) complexity of standard attention. MiniMax H3 is an open-weights omnimodal generation model that can process text, images, video, and audio jointly.
